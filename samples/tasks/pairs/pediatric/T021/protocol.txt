Objective: Identify the ICU patient cohort specified below for a descriptive study.
Design: Retrospective cohort study using MIMIC-IV v3.1.
Population: In MIMIC-IV, take each patient's first ICU stay, restricted to stays where the patient's age at hospital admission is under 18 years. Select the patients who have at least two laboratory platelet measurements <50×10³/µL (counted by distinct measurement times; multiple specimens at the same time count as one) within 48 hours from ICU admission (inclusive of the ICU admission time itself and of the 48-hour mark).
Index time: ICU admission time of the selected ICU stay.
Output: the cohort only (no outcome, no analysis): one row per selected unit with subject_id, hadm_id, stay_id, index_time.
Verdict: status (normal, partial, unanswerable, contradictory, empty_result), reason and limitation.
