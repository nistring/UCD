Objective: Identify the ICU patient cohort specified below for a descriptive study.
Design: Retrospective cohort study using MIMIC-IV v3.1.
Population: In MIMIC-IV, take each patient's first ICU stay, restricted to stays where the patient's age at hospital admission is 18 years or older. Select the patients who have at least one laboratory INR measurement >1.5 within 48 hours from ICU admission (inclusive of the ICU admission time itself and of the 48-hour mark). Then keep only the patients with a documented smoking history of more than 20 pack-years.
Index time: ICU admission time of the selected ICU stay.
Output: the cohort only (no outcome, no analysis): one row per selected unit with subject_id, hadm_id, stay_id, index_time.
Verdict: status (normal, partial, unanswerable, contradictory, empty_result), reason and limitation.
