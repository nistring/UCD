Objective: Identify the ICU patient cohort specified below for a descriptive study.
Design: Retrospective cohort study using MIMIC-IV v3.1.
Population: In MIMIC-IV, take each patient's first ICU stay, restricted to stays where the patient's age at hospital admission is 18 years or older. Select the patients who have at least one laboratory platelet measurement <50×10³/µL within 48 hours from ICU admission (inclusive of the ICU admission time itself and of the 48-hour mark), but exclude patients who had a laboratory platelet measurement <150×10³/µL within the 7 days before ICU admission. For this exclusion, consider all of the patient's laboratory platelet measurements regardless of hospital admission status; the period runs from 7 days before the ICU admission time up to just before the ICU admission time (the admission time itself is not included); patients with no laboratory platelet measurement in this period are not excluded.
Index time: ICU admission time of the selected ICU stay.
Output: the cohort only (no outcome, no analysis): one row per selected unit with subject_id, hadm_id, stay_id, index_time.
Verdict: status (normal, partial, unanswerable, contradictory, empty_result), reason and limitation.
